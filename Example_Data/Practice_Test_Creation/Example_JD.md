### Job Title: Generative AI (GenAI) Developer

**Role Overview:**  
We are seeking a GenAI Developer to design, build, and deploy generative AI solutions using large language models and related technologies.

**Key Responsibilities:**

*   Develop and fine-tune LLM-based applications (e.g., chatbots, copilots, RAG systems)
*   Implement prompt engineering and model evaluation techniques
*   Integrate GenAI solutions with existing systems and APIs
*   Optimize model performance, scalability, and security
*   Collaborate with product, data, and engineering teams

**Required Skills & Qualifications:**

*   Strong proficiency in Python and AI/ML frameworks (PyTorch/TensorFlow)
*   Hands-on experience with LLMs (OpenAI, Azure OpenAI, Hugging Face, etc.)
*   Knowledge of NLP, embeddings, vector databases, and RAG architectures
*   Experience with cloud platforms (Azure/AWS/GCP)
*   Understanding of MLOps and deployment pipelines

**Preferred:**

*   Experience with fine-tuning and custom model training
*   Familiarity with responsible AI and model governance
